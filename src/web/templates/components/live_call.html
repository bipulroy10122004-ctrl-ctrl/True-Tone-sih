🔊 How to Tap & Sniff Remote Caller Voice (WhatsApp Web, Google Meet, Teams): READY TO CAPTURE
1. Click "Start In-Call Audio Interception" below.
2. In the Chrome popup: Select the Chrome Tab where your call is running (WhatsApp Web, Google Meet, or Zoom Web).
3. CRITICAL: Ensure the checkbox at the bottom: "Also share tab audio" is CHECKED ✅ before clicking Share.
4. Desktop Apps (WhatsApp Desktop / Zoom App / Teams): Select "Entire Screen" and check "Share system audio" at bottom left.
