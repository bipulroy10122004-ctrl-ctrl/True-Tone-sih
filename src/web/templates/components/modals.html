Audio Track Required to Tap In-Call Audio

The browser sharing dialog was closed without sharing audio. To capture the voice of the caller on the other side of your call, audio sharing must be enabled:

1. For Web Calls (WhatsApp Web, Google Meet, Zoom Web):
Select the Chrome Tab tab and check "Also share tab audio" at the bottom.

2. For Desktop Apps (WhatsApp Desktop, Teams, Zoom App):
Select Entire Screen and check "Share system audio" at the bottom left.
